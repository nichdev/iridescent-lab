Iridescent

Periwinkle skin, white light drawn along it, and a thin holographic fringe where the light meets the air. Set against warm dune neutrals.

  1. Light is a line, not a fill.Foil lives on edges, hairlines and traces. Never a background.
  2. One glow per screen.The brightest thing is the thing to look at or press.
  3. Warm ground, cool figure.Dune neutrals hold the page so periwinkle reads as presence.

04 · Circuit motif

Light drawn along the form

Lines are generated on a grid with 45° bends only, and end in a ring node. Near the pointer they heat up and send a pulse. Tap on touch screens.

  • Do run lines with the form: from edges, upward, bending 45°.
  • Do keep the core white 1.75px, fringe 7px blurred under it.
  • Do place it on skin or night grounds only.
  • Don't run lines under body text or behind form fields.
  • Don't use more than one live circuit per screen.

Static tile (SVG data URI) for reduced motion and low-power surfaces.

05 · Motion

Motion is light travelling

Things don't bounce or wobble. Light draws in, travels along a path, and settles. With reduced motion on, lines appear fully drawn and pulses are off.

Respond · 120–220 msease-out. Press, hover, toggle.
Arrive · 420 msease-out. Toast, modal, sheet.
Trace · 1.6 sease-in-out. Lines drawing in, pulses. Decorative only.
